🎓 Opportunities & Programs

Rutgers Haskin Shellfish Research Laboratory

In this internship you will test a high-density DNA chip for genomic selection. This chip is expected to reduce the breeding cycle and increase selection efficiency. The skills that you learn during this internship can also be applied in more scenarios that are closer related to agriculture biotechnology.

📍 Location: Port Norris, NJ Visit Website ↗

Rutgers FAME

This is a 12 week program where students can become active participants in scientific discovery and communication. They can interview plant and marine food system scientists and then create engaging videos to teach others.

📍 Location: New Brunswick, NJ Visit Website ↗
