⚖️ REGULATORY BIOTECHNOLOGY OVERVIEW

Welcome to the Regulatory Biotechnology Explorer & Career Hub! Regulatory Biotechnology ensures that all biological products, therapeutics, and genetically engineered crops are safe, effective, and ethically sound for public release and environmental protection.

🏠 HOUSE - WHAT IS IT?

Ensuring biotech products (like genetically engineered crops) are safe for humans & the environment.

🏫 SCHOOL - REQUIRED SKILLS

Understanding regulatory guidelines, strong analytical skills & Bachelor's in Life Sciences.

🏢 OFFICE - JOB OPPORTUNITIES

Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Regulatory Writers, Managers, and CMC Specialists.
