🎓 Opportunities & Programs

NJIT Forensic Science Initiative (FSI) / JumpStart

This program allows students to spend five weeks exploring topics of forensic biology, forensic chemistry, crime scene investigation, forensic law, and field research.

📍 Location: Newark, NJ Visit Website ↗

Passaic County Biotechnology Innovation Center – DNA Learning Center

In this program students entering grades 9 and 10 will learn about criminal investigations, DNA analysis, and crime scene processing. This teaches them to study blood splatter patterns, perform DNA analysis, use microscopes, and more.

📍 Location: Wayne, NJ Visit Website ↗
